How to read this page / Yeh page kaise padhein

Hinglish: Top 1000 coin mein se jo coin ₹500 se mehenga hai, ya jo apne sabse kam price se sabse upar tak 2000% se zyada chadh chuka hai, woh hata diye jaate hain. Baaki coins ki list yahan hai, sabse achhe score wale upar. Kisi bhi coin par tap karo, poori detail aa jayegi. Page kholte hi live price aata hai.

Price
What one coin costs right now, in rupees.
Biggest jump ever
How much the coin went up from its lowest price ever to its highest price ever. 2000% means it went up about 21 times.
Below its peak
How far today's price is under its highest price ever. −70% means it is 70% cheaper than its peak.
Last 30 days
How much the price went up or down in the last month.
Score (0–100)
Higher is better to watch. It looks at coin size, how much it is traded, how far below its peak it is, recent trend and price calmness.
Strong Watch / Watch
Score 80+ / 65–79. Most interesting.
Monitor / Weak
Score 50–64 / below 50.

This is not financial advice. The score only sorts coins. It does not predict prices. A low price does not mean a coin is cheap. Always research before investing.
