How to read this page / Yeh page kaise padhein

Hinglish: Top 1000 coin mein se jo coin ₹500 se mehenga hai, ya jo kabhi bhi neeche se upar 2000% se zyada chadh chuka hai, woh hata diye jaate hain. Baaki coins ki list yahan hai, sabse achhe score wale upar. Kisi bhi coin par tap karo, poori detail aa jayegi. Price har 3 ghante mein apne aap update hota hai.

Price
What one coin costs right now, in rupees.
Biggest jump ever
The biggest rise that really happened: from a low price up to a later high price. 2000% means it went up about 21 times. ⚠ means older price history was not available, so an earlier, bigger jump may be missing.
Below its peak
How far today's price is under its highest price ever. −70% means it is 70% cheaper than its peak.
Last 30 days
How much the price went up or down in the last month.
Score (0–100)
Higher is better to watch. It looks at coin size, how much it is traded, how far below its peak it is, recent trend and price calmness.
